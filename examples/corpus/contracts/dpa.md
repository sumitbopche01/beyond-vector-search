# Data Processing Addendum

## Page 1

## 1. Roles

Northwind Labs is the processor. The customer is the controller. This addendum is incorporated into the Master Services Agreement.

## 4. Personal-data incidents

The processor notifies the customer's security contact and the CFO within 72 hours of confirming a personal-data breach.

The notice states what happened, which records were involved, and what the processor has done so far. Liability for the incident follows the MSA, including any carve-out that the MSA applies to data protection.

## 5. Subprocessors

A list of subprocessors is available on request. The customer may object within ten business days of a new subprocessor being named.
