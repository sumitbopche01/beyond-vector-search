# Enterprise Addendum

## Page 1

## 1. Scope

This addendum applies to customers on an Enterprise Order Form. It modifies the Master Services Agreement. If the documents conflict, this addendum wins on fees and on the cap, and the MSA wins on everything else.

## 3. Liability cap

For enterprise customers, the aggregate liability cap is 2x annual fees, except as set out in MSA §9.3.

The CFO countersigns enterprise orders above $500,000.

## 4. Support

Enterprise customers get a named support lead. Uptime credits, if any, are in the Service Level Agreement and do not change the cap above.
