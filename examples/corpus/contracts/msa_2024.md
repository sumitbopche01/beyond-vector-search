# Master Services Agreement

## Page 1

## 1. Parties

This Master Services Agreement ("MSA") is between Northwind Labs ("Provider") and the customer named on an Order Form. Order Forms, addenda, and the Data Processing Addendum are part of this MSA.

## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 Confidential Information

Confidential Information means non-public business, product, and security information disclosed by either party. It does not include information that is public through no fault of the receiving party.

## 4. Fees

Fees are set on the Order Form. Enterprise fees are modified by the Enterprise Addendum when one is in force.

## 9. Liability

### 9.1 Limitation

Each party's aggregate liability under this MSA is limited to the fees paid in the twelve months before the claim, except where Section 9.2 or Section 9.3 says otherwise.

### 9.2 Different cap on an order

The cap in Section 9.1 does not apply where an Order Form or addendum sets a different cap. Read that document for the number, then come back here for what the cap does not cover.

### 9.3 Carve-outs

Section 9.1 and any cap stated in an addendum do not apply to:

- breaches of confidentiality under Section 2.1
- breaches of data protection obligations under the Data Processing Addendum
- a party's fraud or wilful misconduct

The Chief Financial Officer (CFO) must approve any settlement above the applicable cap.

## 10. Term

The MSA renews annually unless either party gives notice 30 days before the renewal date.
