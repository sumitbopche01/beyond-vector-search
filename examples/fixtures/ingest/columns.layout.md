# Page sketch — two columns, one page

This is the page a PDF extractor will see. It is not a document the agent should search.

Left column, top to bottom:

- Heading: Client
- Client means the paying customer named on the order form.

Right column, top to bottom:

- Heading: Client
- Client means the implementation partner who delivers the work.

A reader who finishes the left column before starting the right one gets two definitions. A reader who takes line 1 from both columns, then line 2 from both, joins the paying customer to the implementation partner.
